ECHOMIND DESKTOP: PHASE 09, STABILISE (LONG RECORDINGS AND BUG FIXES)
================================================================================
Status:      COMPLETE. Recordings of 1 to 3 hours work end to end on both free providers.
Prompt refs: §5 long audio; §22 reliability; §25 testing; §21 privacy (logging)
Date:        2026-11-23


1. OBJECTIVE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The owner asked that 1, 2 or even 3-hour recordings "shouldn't be a problem", then asked for
every bug to be fixed. This phase proves a 3-hour recording live, fixes everything that
proof exposed, and fixes the problems a full visual audit of the app found.


2. WHAT CHANGED (AS A USER SEES IT)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Long recordings
- A 3-hour recording transcribes, summarizes, gets chapters, a title, minutes, a quiz,
  tasks and chat answers. Nothing needs babysitting: free-tier waits, network drops and
  app restarts are all handled, and finished work is never paid for twice.
- Titles of long recordings describe the whole recording, not just its first topic.
- Chat questions survive a short network drop or rate limit (up to three quiet retries).

Reliability
- A slow or stalled connection can no longer hang a job: every request has a time limit.
- A job that keeps making progress is never stopped for "too many failures"; only six
  failures in a row with no progress stop it.
- Groq's reasoning model no longer returns empty replies (see 4, F1).
- If EchoMind's window ever crashes, it reopens by itself and says so. Jobs keep running.
- EchoMind keeps a small diagnostics log (data folder > logs > echomind.log) of starts,
  quits, crashes and errors, with file paths and keys removed, so an unexpected close
  always leaves a reason.

Screens
- The Language choice in Summaries, Documents and AI Studio is now a normal dropdown with
  the full list, plus "Other language…" to type any language. The old text box hid the
  list behind its own value.
- Library rows no longer waste a column on the selection checkbox: it appears over the
  recording's icon on hover or in selection mode, so rows line up with Home.
- The development window shows the EchoMind icon instead of Electron's.


3. KEY DECISIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
D1  Notes on long transcripts are merged in rounds (reduceNotes) until they fit one
    request, and every merge is saved, so a rate-limit pause never repeats work.
D2  "Failures in a row", not "failures in total": a 3-hour job on a flaky connection
    may see dozens of blips and still be healthy.
D3  Reasoning models are asked for low effort rather than given bigger budgets: bigger
    budgets cost free-tier tokens per minute, and EchoMind's tasks need little reasoning.
    Only Groq's gpt-oss and Gemini's thinking models get the field; unknown services get
    nothing extra, because strict services refuse unknown fields.
D4  A crashed window is reloaded, not left blank and not used as a reason to quit.
D5  The live-test cache moved out of test-results (Playwright empties it on every run;
    it wiped a transcribed 3-hour library once).


4. BUGS FOUND AND FIXED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
F1  Groq summary of a 3-hour recording never got past part 1. Captured live: gpt-oss-120b
    spent 1,498 of 1,500 tokens on hidden reasoning and returned no text, 77 times in a
    row. Fixed with reasoning_effort "low" (now about 17 to 430 tokens of reasoning), a
    retry with twice the room if a reply is still all reasoning, and 2,000 tokens for
    notes (1,500 cut dense parts off mid-sentence).
F2  Long Groq jobs failed after 6 network errors even while progressing. Fixed (D2).
F3  Groq's "Failed to validate JSON" ended chapter jobs. Now asked once more, then retried
    by the queue.
F4  Titles of long recordings named only the first topic. Prompt and sampling fixed.
F5  Chat failed on a single rate limit or network blip. Now retried quietly.
F6  A stalled connection could hang a job forever. Every request now has a time limit.
F7  The app closed itself once, about 4 seconds after a Groq summary started on the
    3-hour recording. It could not be made to happen again in three runs of 1 to 3
    minutes each, with every quit and window path instrumented. The fixes above remove
    the conditions it was seen in, a crashed window now reopens instead of closing, and
    the new log records any start, quit, crash or uncaught error, so a repeat leaves
    evidence. Reported honestly as not reproduced, not as proven fixed.
F8  Language boxes hid their own list; Library rows wasted a column; the dev window had
    the wrong icon. Fixed (section 2).


5. TESTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Unit: 270 passing (new: merging notes for 3 hours, request timeouts, JSON retry, chat
  retries, reasoning-only replies, reasoning options per model, the log file's scrubbing
  and rollover, a 3-hour flaky-connection transcription).
- End to end: 50 passing, 1 skipped (the AMR-without-FFmpeg case, which steps aside when the add-on is installed). New: a crashed window reopens with a
  notice and the log records start, crash and quit.
- Live, 3-hour meeting of 30 sessions (tests/live/long.spec.ts):
  Stage 1 (Groq Whisper): imported in 84 s, 19 parts, all 30 sessions' facts found,
  timing drift under 1 s at every session start; a job killed in part 14 resumed and
  finished parts 14 to 19 after reopening.
  Stage 2: see section 6.


6. LIVE RESULTS, STAGE 2 (3-HOUR TRANSCRIPT)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Gemini (gemini-3.5-flash-lite), 2026-11-22: every task (summary, chapters against the real
  session starts, title, minutes with PDF and Word, quiz, 12 tasks with owners, chat) ran
  on the whole 3-hour transcript in one request each, quickly and correctly. (The detailed
  report file was lost when test-results was emptied by a later run; see D5.)

Groq (gpt-oss-120b), 2026-11-22 and 23:
- First runs exposed F1 to F5. After the fixes, the 3-hour summary read all 16 parts and
  made all 7 merges (each saved), reaching 94%, about 30 seconds per part under the free
  8,000 tokens a minute.
- It then reached Groq's free 200,000 tokens a day, which the day's diagnosis runs had
  mostly used. EchoMind did exactly what it should: it explained the wait, kept every
  finished part, and retried on Groq's schedule. The run was stopped there rather than
  waiting hours; its saved progress is kept in the test cache, and the remaining Groq
  stage 2 tasks will be rerun after the daily window resets (during Phase 12, which needs
  live Groq anyway).
- Conclusion for users, recorded in FREE_STACK.md: on Groq's free tier a 3-hour summary
  takes about 10 minutes and 40% of a day's tokens, so running every tool on one 3-hour
  recording can take more than a day. Gemini handles very long recordings in minutes.


7. REVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Designer: the language dropdown now behaves like every other choice; the Library row's
left edge matches Home's. Long jobs explain each wait in plain words.
Developer: every new failure path has a unit test; the reasoning fields are confined to
the two providers verified live; the log writes only rare events and can never crash the
app (all writes are guarded).
Open: Gemini's free models answered "high demand" (HTTP 503) for much of this session;
EchoMind retries, and Phase 12 will add switching to another model when one is overloaded.
