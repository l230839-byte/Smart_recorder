1. OBJECTIVE

2. WHAT THIS PHASE INCLUDES (THE FEATURES, AS A USER SEES THEM)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
AI Studio (every tile now works)
- Smart Summarizer and Lecture Notes (Phase 4), Meeting Minutes, PDF, Word and
  Presentations (Phase 5), and, new here:
- Flashcards: 3 to 60 cards, four difficulty settings, any language.
- Quiz Generator: multiple choice, true or false, short and long questions, chosen per quiz.
- Mind Map: a central topic, branches and points, each pointing at its moment.
- Study Notes: study guide, revision sheet, topic-wise lecture notes, definitions, formulas,
  important concepts, simple explanation, detailed explanation.
- Translation Studio: a transcript line by line with its times, or a saved summary.
- Comparison: 2 to 6 recordings; shared themes, differences, decisions over time, open
  questions, each point naming its recording.
- Rewrite Studio: report, article, email, explanation, blog post or formal record.
- Custom Studio: your own instructions.
- Any tool's options can be saved as a template and reused.
- Opened from a recording ("More in AI Studio"), each tool starts with that recording.
- Every result is kept: listed in AI Studio and on each recording, renamable, deletable, and
  "Make another like this".
- PDF or Word: any result becomes an editable document (Phase 5) and exports. Quizzes export
  with an answer key; flashcards as a table; mind maps as an outline.

Studying
- Flashcards: study one card at a time; Space flips, K "I knew it", L "still learning",
  arrows move. At the end, study again only the cards still to learn. Shuffle; see all.
- Quizzes: answer, then "Check answers". Multiple choice and true or false are marked
  automatically, with the right option highlighted; written answers show the model answer
  and you mark your own. Explanations appear; any that go beyond the recording are labelled
  "AI explanation, beyond the recording". Every attempt is saved with its score.
- Every card, question and map point has a time that plays the moment it came from.

Tasks
- "Find action items" on a recording, or the Action Items tool, finds tasks, due dates
  (worked out from the recording date, so "Friday" becomes a date), owners and priority, only
  when the recording says so.
- They wait in "To review" with the words they came from and a time to play. Approve,
  edit and approve, dismiss, or approve all. Dismissed ones are not suggested again; finding
  again adds no duplicates.
- "To do" lists approved and your own tasks, soonest due first, with overdue ones marked.
  Tick to complete, with Undo. "Done" keeps the history. Search across everything.
- Reminders at a time you choose appear as Windows notifications; selecting one opens
  Tasks. Home shows how many are to do, overdue and to review.
- Nothing is sent to a calendar or to anyone (§14).

Several recordings at once (workflow D)
- In the Library, select recordings, then Process: a summary, study guide, flashcards,
  quiz or action items for each, or one combined result. Each runs in the queue with its
  own progress and retry.


3. KEY DECISIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
D1  One engine for every tool. Lines are numbered S1, S2… across all chosen recordings and
    split into pieces that fit the provider. Structured tools ask each piece for JSON with
    the count shared out by size; written tools take notes per piece, then combine.
D2  Every item is validated before it is kept: a real source line, a multiple-choice answer
    that is one of four distinct options (letters and indexes are mapped back), true or
    false only, real dates only, mind maps limited to three levels and 80 points. Bad
    replies are asked for once more.
D3  Each finished piece is stored, so a failure or a rate limit never repeats finished work.
D4  Grounding in study material: answers must come from the recording; explanations may
    add outside knowledge but are flagged, and the flag is shown and exported (§13).
D5  Tasks are proposals until approved. Approved tasks are the user's: if their recording
    is deleted, they stay, with the link and quoted words removed. Unapproved ones go.
D6  Reminders are checked every 30 seconds while EchoMind runs, and once at launch, so
    reminders missed while it was closed appear when it opens. Each shows once.
D7  Export reuses Phase 5 documents rather than a second exporter, so study material gets
    the same fonts, Urdu shaping, layout options and formats.

7. UNRESOLVED / KNOWN LIMITS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
L1  NOT YET RUN ON LIVE GROQ OR GEMINI. `npm run test:live` now also makes a quiz and finds
    action items in the generated meeting, and checks them against its known facts.
L2  Reminders appear only while EchoMind is running (or when it next opens). A background
    reminder service would need EchoMind to start with Windows; that is a Phase 8 choice.
L3  Mind maps of long recordings read an even selection, so a brief topic may be missing.
L4  Written quiz answers are marked by the learner, not by AI, so the score depends on
    honest self-marking. AI marking is possible later but would send answers to a provider.
L5  Everything else from earlier reports still applies.
