ECHOMIND DESKTOP: PHASE 13, OFFLINE TRANSCRIPTION
================================================================================
Status:      COMPLETE.
Prompt refs: §0 free stack and local-first; §5 transcription; §21 privacy
Date:        2026-11-23


1. OBJECTIVE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Transcription that needs no internet, no key and no one's servers, chosen by the owner
as part of the AI model upgrades. The whole AI pipeline after transcription already
works the same whatever made the transcript.


2. WHAT CHANGED (AS A USER SEES IT)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Settings > AI providers > Transcription > On this computer (offline Whisper)
- Choose a model and press Download, once:
    Tiny 31 MB, Base 57 MB, Small 181 MB (recommended), Medium 514 MB,
    Large v3 Turbo 547 MB.
  All understand many languages, including Urdu, Hindi and Arabic.
- A progress bar shows the download; Pause keeps what has arrived, and the button then
  says "Continue (… MB done)". Downloaded models can be deleted to free space.
- After that, transcription runs entirely on the computer, with Offline only on. No key,
  no agreement screen, nothing sent.
- If Offline only is on when you press Download, EchoMind asks first, because the
  download itself contacts Hugging Face (nothing from your recordings is sent).
- Choosing this provider before downloading a model explains what to do instead of
  asking for a key.


3. KEY DECISIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
D1  whisper.cpp (MIT) as a separate program, not a native Node module: nothing to
    compile, crashes cannot take EchoMind down, and cancelling simply ends the process.
D2  Pinned to release b5454 (public for seven weeks when chosen) and checked against
    the SHA-256 GitHub publishes for the file; its MIT licence is fetched, checked and
    shipped beside it. Only whisper-cli and its libraries are kept.
D3  Models are downloaded by the person, not bundled: the installer stays its size,
    and people choose speed or accuracy. Every model is the quantized multilingual
    file, checked against its published SHA-256 and only renamed for use once it
    matches; interrupted downloads continue (HTTP Range).
D4  Pieces for whisper.cpp are written by the existing media worker as 16 kHz mono WAV,
    so offline transcription does not depend on the optional FFmpeg add-on.
D5  whisper.cpp's default language is English, so EchoMind always passes "auto" unless
    a language is chosen; otherwise Urdu would be transcribed as English.
D6  All but one CPU core (at most 8), so recording and the window stay responsive.
D7  "Local" already meant "skip key, consent and Offline only" in the privacy gate; the
    gate now also waits until the chosen model is on disk.


4. TESTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Unit (286 passing): reading whisper-cli JSON (times, language, non-speech tags,
clamping, silence); the model catalogue (multilingual, hashes, Small first); download,
hash check and final rename; resume from a part file with a Range request; a tampered
file deleted; a failed connection keeping its part; offline provider needing no key,
consent or internet but waiting for its model, with 16 kHz WAV pieces.

Live, in the real app with Offline only on (tests/live/local-whisper.spec.ts),
2026-11-23, 70 s two-voice meeting:
                 Download   Speed               Facts
  Tiny (31 MB)   6.8 s      11.5x real time     11 of 12
  Small (181 MB) 31.4 s     2.2x real time      11 of 12
Both missed only a spelling ("Lena" for "Lina"); line times agree with Gemini and
Groq to within about 0.3 s. On this computer Small would take about 80 minutes for a
3-hour recording.

Installer: rebuilt (196 MB, with FFmpeg and whisper.cpp); the packaged-app test now also
confirms offline Whisper is present in the installed app, and passes.

Groq 3-hour rerun (carried over from Phase 9): Groq's per-minute limit had reset, but its
daily token limit had not. The summary saved 3 more merges (26 parts in all) and then
waited on the daily limit again, as designed. This confirms FREE_STACK.md: on Groq's free
tier, every AI tool on a 3-hour recording cannot finish in one day; Gemini can.
During the run, EchoMind's window was closed after 9.5 minutes. The new diagnostics log
shows an orderly close of the window itself (window-close, then quit, exit 0), the
pattern of the close button or Alt+F4, not a crash or a quit by the app. The test windows
open on the desktop, and the owner confirmed closing them by hand, both this one and the
"app quit by itself" of Phase 9. There is no self-quit bug.

The whisper.cpp download script was run from a clean folder: hash checked, licence
checked, and the options EchoMind uses confirmed in the tool's own help.


5. REVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Designer: offline is now a first-class choice beside the cloud services, with sizes
and trade-offs in plain words, and the one moment it touches the network is explicit.
Developer: no new npm dependency; the provider and the model store are small and fully
unit-tested; the binary is a build asset like FFmpeg.
Limits: speed depends on the computer, and there is no GPU acceleration (the CPU build
is used so every Windows PC works). No speaker labels offline yet: a candidate is
sherpa-onnx (Apache-2.0) speaker diarization.
