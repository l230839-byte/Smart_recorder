ECHOMIND DESKTOP: PHASE 17, A NEW ECHOMIND MARK                (version 0.5.0)
================================================================================
Status:      COMPLETE.
Prompt refs: §18 design (brand)
Date:        2026-11-24


1. OBJECTIVE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The owner asked for a better logo and chose to keep the name EchoMind. Find a mark that
comes from the name itself and stays clear from the taskbar to the installer.


2. WHAT CHANGED (AS A USER SEES IT)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- The "echo quote": a bold quotation mark followed by its fainter echo. What was said,
  and what EchoMind keeps of it.
- In the sidebar, welcome and lock screens it takes the accent colour (and black and
  white in high contrast); the app icon, taskbar, Start menu and installer use indigo.


3. KEY DECISIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
D1  Three new concepts (Echo quote, Ripple e, Mind wave) were rendered beside the
    current Level E at 150, 64, 32, 24 and 16 px, light and dark, and shown to the owner
    before anything changed. The owner chose the Echo quote.
D2  Its shapes are solid, not thin strokes, so every icon size renders cleanly from the
    one drawing; the hand-placed pixel versions the Level E needed are gone. Below 32 px
    the mark is drawn 12% larger to use the few pixels well (checked enlarged).
D3  One drawing (QUOTE_PATH in Shell.tsx, the same path in scripts/make-icon.mjs).


4. TESTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Icon sizes extracted from icon.ico and checked enlarged at 16, 20, 24, 32 and 48 px.
- The mark checked in the app: light, dark, rose accent, high contrast, welcome screen.
- Accessibility (every accent and high contrast) and foundation tests pass; unit tests,
  type check and lint pass; the installer is rebuilt and its packaged test passes.


5. REVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Designer: the mark reads as speech before anyone sees the name, and the echo ties it to
the name; it has no lookalike among common recorder or chat icons.
Developer: one path, no pixel tables to keep in step.
