ECHOMIND DESKTOP: PHASE 10, LOGO AND ICONS
================================================================================
Status:      COMPLETE.
Prompt refs: §18 design (brand, consistency)
Date:        2026-11-23


1. OBJECTIVE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Give EchoMind a mark of its own, sharp at every size from the taskbar to the installer,
and make the app's icons say something: what kind of tool each one is.


2. WHAT CHANGED (AS A USER SEES IT)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- The new mark, chosen by the owner from three concepts: the "Level E", an E built from
  three audio-level bars with an echo dot. It is a monogram and an equalizer at once, and
  stays readable at 16 px. In the sidebar, onboarding and lock screen it follows the
  theme's accent; the app icon is a fixed indigo.
- The taskbar, Start menu, Explorer and installer use a real multi-size Windows icon.
- AI Studio groups its 15 tools by purpose, each group with its own colour:
    Summaries and writing (accent)  Study (teal)  Meetings and tasks (ember)
    Documents and slides (neutral)
- Home's Ask card uses the AI colour (teal), as Ask AI does elsewhere.


3. KEY DECISIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
D1  Three concepts were drawn and rendered at 160, 64, 32, 24 and 16 px on light and
    dark before choosing; "Wave to words" was the most literal but blurred at 16 px.
D2  The 16, 20, 24 and 32 px icons are placed on whole pixels by hand. Scaled vector
    drawings put each bar between two pixels and the E turned grey (checked enlarged).
D3  The icon file holds PNG images, which Windows has read since Vista; no new tool or
    dependency was needed to build it.
D4  Icon colours come only from theme tokens (one shared .icon-tile with four tones), so
    the coming accent and high-contrast themes recolour them with no further change.
D5  Groups in Studio are real headings, so screen readers can jump between them.


4. TESTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Accessibility (axe, WCAG 2.1 AA, light and dark), foundation and Studio end-to-end
  tests pass. Unit tests, type check and lint pass.
- Icon sizes extracted from icon.ico and checked enlarged at 16, 20, 24, 32 and 48 px.
- Screens reviewed by eye in light and dark: Home, Library, AI Studio.


5. REVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Designer: the mark is simple enough to recognise at a glance and has no lookalike among
common recorder or chat apps. Studio is easier to scan with four labelled groups.
Developer: one drawing (BRAND_PATH) is shared by the in-app mark; the icon script is the
only place with pixel-placed sizes and says so. No new dependencies.
