# EchoMind user guide

EchoMind turns recordings into transcripts, summaries, documents, study material and tasks. It runs on
your computer, works with free AI services using your own key, and sends nothing anywhere until you allow it.

## First steps

1. **Set up AI (optional).** Settings > AI providers. Get a free key from Groq (console.groq.com/keys) and paste it there. Without a key, choose the Demo provider to try everything; its results are labelled as demo text.
   For transcripts that say who spoke, choose **Google Gemini Transcribe** with a free key from aistudio.google.com/apikey. Google may use free-tier audio to improve its products, so keep confidential recordings on Groq or offline.
2. **Turn off Offline only** in Settings > Privacy when you are ready to use a cloud provider. EchoMind asks for your agreement before the first upload to each one.
3. **Record or import.** Record from the Record tab, or import files from your phone's recorder, WhatsApp voice notes or videos (Home > Import, or drag files onto the window). Every recording gets the same features, however it arrived.

## Every recording

- **Transcribe** on the recording's page. Edit any line; the original is kept.
- **Summaries**: choose a format, length, tone and language. Make as many as you like; nothing is transcribed again.
- **Ask AI**: questions answered from the recording, with times you can play. If it is not in the recording, EchoMind says so.
- **Documents**: nine templates, an editor with a live page preview, and export to PDF, Word, PowerPoint, HTML, Markdown or text.
- **AI Studio**: flashcards, quizzes, mind maps, study notes, translations, comparisons, rewrites and your own instructions.
- **Find action items**: tasks found in the recording wait in Tasks for your approval.

## Finding things

- **Search** (Ctrl+K) looks through names, transcripts, chapters, speakers, summaries and documents.
- **Ask AI** from Home or AI Studio asks across your whole library, a collection or chosen recordings.

## Making it yours

- **Appearance**: Settings > Appearance. Light, dark or follow Windows; six accent colours (indigo, teal, rose, amber, green, slate); and high contrast, which uses black and white with strong outlines. Contrast set to System switches to high contrast whenever Windows asks for more contrast.

## Keeping your library safe

- **App lock**: Settings > Security and backup > App lock. A PIN when EchoMind opens, after idle time, and when Windows locks. A forgotten PIN cannot be recovered.
- **Backups**: Settings > Backup and restore. An encrypted copy of everything except API keys, saved where you choose, such as a USB drive or a cloud-synced folder. Keep the password safe: it cannot be reset.
- **Sharing**: on a recording, More > Share as a bundle. The file carries the audio, transcript, summaries, chapters and documents. Anyone with EchoMind can import it from Home > Import. Bundles are not encrypted, so share them as you would the recording itself.

## Keyboard

| Keys | Does |
| --- | --- |
| Ctrl+K | Search everything |
| Space | Play or pause (flip a card in flashcards) |
| Alt+Left | Back |
| Ctrl+B, Ctrl+I, Ctrl+S | Bold, italic and save in the document editor |
| K, L | "I knew it", "still learning" in flashcards |

## Limits worth knowing

- Transcription and AI need a free provider key and an internet connection; recording, import, playback, editing and search work offline.
- In PDFs, Urdu displays correctly but only part of it can be copied out as text. Word and text exports keep it exactly.
- Task reminders appear while EchoMind is running. Turn on "Start with Windows" so it is.
